## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Extremely slow locked camera from a low position. The framing tightens by a few
centimetres only - the camera is patient, not dramatic.

THE FATHER STANDS STILL FOR FOUR FULL SECONDS. Breathing. Then his knees give and he
sits down on the kerb in ONE motion - not a collapse, just emptiness leaving his legs.

HE FOLDS FORWARD, both hands going into his hair, and holds. His shoulders shake twice,
silently. HIS FACE IS NEVER VISIBLE - not at any point - it stays turned down and away
from the lens, buried against his own forearms.

THE MANILA FOLDER SLIPS from his grip and settles flat on the pavement beside him, one
corner lifting in the breeze.

DUST drifts through the last of the orange light. The street is entirely empty.
```
