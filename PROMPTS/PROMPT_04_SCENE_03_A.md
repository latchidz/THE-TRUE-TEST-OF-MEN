## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera, low in the aisle, slow push-in of no more than a step.

AMIN takes the last two steps to the counter. His free hand lifts off his thigh and
opens, palm up - the beginning of a sentence, not the middle of one. He stops.

YOUSSEF looks up from the counter. For one beat his head is level. Then it breaks: he
understands, and he does not want to. HE TURNS HIS HEAD AWAY, to his right and down,
so his eyes leave Amin's entirely, and his right hand comes up and presses flat on his
own chest over his heart, fingers spread. His lips move once, briefly, and close.

AYM IN THE FOREGROUND: his shoulder stays motionless, a dark edge on the near side of
the frame, breathing.

THE PUSH-IN settles on Youssef's face at around second eight and holds there.
```
