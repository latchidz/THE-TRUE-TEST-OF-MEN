## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera outside the doorway. The interior does the work.

HAMZA does not lift his head for three seconds. Then he wipes his hands down the front
of his apron, comes round the end of the counter - slow, heavy - and stops in front of
Amin.

HE DOES NOT PUT HIS HAND ON AMIN'S SHOULDER AT FIRST. He stands with his arms at his
sides and says the sentence. Then, as it lands, HIS RIGHT ARM COMES UP AND HIS OPEN HAND
PRESSES FLAT ON AMIN'S SHOULDER, fingers spread, thumb forward - and he keeps it there.

HAMZA'S HEAD IS TURNED AWAY AND DOWN the whole time, his eyes on the floor beside his
friend, never on his face, never on the lens.

AMIN's head bows lower. The medical folder in his lowered hand stays still.

ONE BARE BULB sways slightly above them, so the shadows move half a centimetre.
```
