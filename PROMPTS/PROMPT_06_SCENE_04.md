## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera, eye level, from the roller door. Nothing moves except the men.

RAMI freezes for a full beat, his hand still in the drawer. His eyes flick from the
money to Amin and back to the money. Then HIS HAND COMES DOWN AND PUSHES THE DRAWER
SHUT - one firm, final motion - and he wipes his palm on his hip, not lifting his eyes.

AMIN's lifted hand hangs in the air for a second too long, then falls slowly to his
side. He does not move his feet.

SILENCE HOLDS for three full seconds. Then Rami's jaw works and his shoulders lift in
a small shrug that is not an apology.

DUST drifts through the daylight coming in from the roller door.
```
