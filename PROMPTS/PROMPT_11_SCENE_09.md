## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera, low, from behind the seated men. The foreground does not move; the
background does.

AMIN STEPS THROUGH THE DOOR and stops. His head is down. He does not lift it. The white
corridor light blows out behind him and he is a dark shape in it.

IN THE FOREGROUND the four silhouettes BEGIN TO TURN - not together: Hamza first, a slow
lift of the chin; then Rami; then Karim; and Youssef last, his whole head coming round
in one motion.

THEN THEY RISE. Not all at once: chairs scrape one after another, four men coming up
out of plastic seats into a loose half-circle, none of them dressed for it, none of them
knowing what to do with their hands.

AMIN still has not looked up.

THE TUBES above do not flicker.
```
