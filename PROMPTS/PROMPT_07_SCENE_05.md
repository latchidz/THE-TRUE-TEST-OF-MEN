## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera at the glass door, slow push-in down the shop.

KARIM does not look up for the first three seconds. His fingers keep working the phone,
turning it over. Then AMIN's voice reaches him and his hands stop.

HE LOOKS UP ONCE - a quick lift of the chin - and meets Amin's eyes for less than a
second. Then he puts the phone down flat on the glass, and HIS HEAD GOES AWAY: down and
to his left, past the edge of the frame, at the floor. He does not bring it back. His
jaw tightens and his hands come off the counter and go into his pockets.

AMIN's shoulder in the near foreground does not move.

THE FLUORESCENT TUBES stay constant - no flicker, no drama. The push-in ends on Karim's
averted face.
```
