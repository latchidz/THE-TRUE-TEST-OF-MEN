## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera behind the counter. The shot widens as the camera falls back a step -
the perspective opens rather than cuts.

YOUSSEF stays exactly where he is for two full seconds. Then his hand slides off his
chest and hangs at his side.

AMIN turns away without another word and walks down the aisle toward the glass door.
His shoulders drop with each step and his head lowers. He does not look back. The
medical folder swings once against his thigh.

YOUSSEF TURNS HIS HEAD to follow him - a slow quarter turn - and holds there until
Amin is a silhouette against the blue dusk coming through the glass.

The bell on the door shivers once. The blue dusk light spills across the tiled floor.
```
